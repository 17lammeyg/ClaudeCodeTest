Client: NordHome AB
Situation: NordHome is a mid-size Swedish home furnishings retailer with 120 stores across Scandinavia and annual revenues of approximately €1.4B. The company has saturated its core Nordic markets and is exploring international expansion. The CEO and board have identified Southeast Asia — specifically Vietnam, Indonesia, and Thailand — as priority markets given the region's growing middle class and rising discretionary spending. NordHome has no existing presence in Asia and has never operated outside of Europe. They have engaged Xenove to advise on market entry strategy and are looking for a recommendation within 90 days.
Key tensions: The board is split between a capital-light franchise model and a directly-operated store model. There is also pressure to move quickly as a European competitor (not named) is rumored to be evaluating the same markets.
Our ask: Structure the key questions we need to answer to develop a market entry recommendation, and outline how we would approach this engagement.
