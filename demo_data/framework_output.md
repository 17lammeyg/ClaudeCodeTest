## 3. Slide 1 — Situation

**NordHome faces a strategic inflection point**

NordHome has built a €1.4B business across 120 stores in Scandinavia — a position of strength in markets that are now structurally saturated. Organic growth in the Nordics is limited to low single digits, and the board has identified Southeast Asia as the platform for the company's next chapter.

The region is compelling: Vietnam, Indonesia, and Thailand collectively represent a home furnishings market growing at 8–12% annually, driven by rapid urbanization and an expanding middle class with rising aspirations for quality home design.

The strategic question is not whether to enter — it is **how, where first, and with what model.** The board is divided between a capital-light franchise approach and a directly-operated store model. A European competitor is reportedly evaluating the same markets, adding urgency.
